How it works

Jot a few words. Get the meeting, written up.

1

It listens on two channels

Your microphone and your Mac's system audio are captured separately, so “Me” and “Them” never get mixed up. Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack: no bot, no invite, no plugin.

2

It transcribes as you talk

Qwen3-ASR runs on Apple's GPU through MLX and puts text on screen a fraction of a second after it's spoken. It reads your meeting's title, attendees and glossary to get names right.

3

It enhances your notes

When the call ends, a local LLM merges what you wrote with what was said. Your words stay black, its additions are grey, and the ⌕ on every line opens the exact moment in the transcript.

Privacy, by construction

Your meetings are yours. Not training data.

Every model runs on your machine. Transcripts and notes live in a local SQLite file you can open, back up or delete. The only network call is the one-time model download from Hugging Face.

  • No account nothing to sign up for
  • No uploads audio never leaves the Mac
  • No telemetry not even crash reports
  • No subscription MIT licensed
What runs on your Mac

Small models, measured on real work.

About 3.5 GB in total, downloaded once from inside the app and checked against their SHA-256. Every change to the pipeline is measured against scripted meetings and logged, kept or thrown out.
